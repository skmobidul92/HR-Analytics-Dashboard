# HR Analytics Dashboard ( Employee Attrition & Workforce Analysis )

# Project Objective
The main objective of this HR Analytics Dashboard is to analyze employee data and provide actionable insights into employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary, and departmental trends.

## Business Question & KPIs
- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which age group has the highest employee attrition?
- Which education field has the highest attrition rate?
- Which salary slab has the highest number of employees leaving?
- Which department has the highest attrition?
- Which job roles experience the highest attrition?
- Does salary level have an impact on employee attrition?
- Does employee tenure/years at company influence attrition?
- What is the average monthly income of employees?
- Are there noticeable differences in attrition across employee demographics such as gender?

# Process
- Collected employee HR data and cleaned missing, duplicate, and inconsistent records.
- Created useful categories such as Age Group and Salary Slab, and prepared the data for analysis.
- Calculated key HR metrics including Total Employee Count, Attrition Rate, and Average Monthly Income.
- nalyzed employee attrition by education, age group, salary slab, job role, department, and years at company.
- Built an interactive Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard with slicers for Department, Gender, and other HR dimensions to identify attrition patterns and support HR decision-making.
